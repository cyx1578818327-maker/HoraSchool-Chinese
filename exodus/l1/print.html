三、讲解一 · 这些是名字

要点 希伯来文书名取自开头“这些是名字”；书名“出埃及”来自希腊文译本，意思是“出去、离开”。出埃及记接着创世记：雅各一家七十人下到埃及。书里几次说摩西把耶和华的话写下来（出 17:14；24:4），主耶稣称这几卷书为“摩西的书”（可 12:26）。真正的主角不是摩西。

六、想一想

复述（不看书）：出埃及记分哪三段？每段一句话讲了什么？神做这一切是为了什么？至少用上：拯救、立约、同在、“带来归我”。

参考：1–18 拯救，神把百姓从为奴之家救出来；19–24 立约，在西乃山成为神的百姓；25–40 同在，神吩咐造会幕，荣光充满帐幕。神说救他们是要“带来归我”。

辨析：有同学说：“摩西救了以色列人，所以摩西就是基督。”他说得对吗？

参考：不对。摩西是神使用的人，他领百姓出埃及是基督更大拯救的影儿；影儿像真实的，却不是它本身。摩西自己也需要神的拯救（出 4:24–26），也没有进迦南。

3. Talk 1 · These are the names

Key points The Hebrew title comes from the opening words, “These are the names”; “Exodus” comes from the Greek translation and means “a going out, departure”. Exodus continues Genesis: Jacob’s family of seventy went down to Egypt. The book says Moses wrote down the Lord’s words (Ex 17:14; 24:4), and Jesus called these books “the book of Moses” (Mk 12:26). The real main character is not Moses.

4. Talk 2 · Three stages

Key points Chapters 1–18: rescue from the house of slavery. In 19:1 they reach Mount Sinai and stay there for the rest of the book — nearly a year (Num 10:11–12). Chapters 19–24: covenant — they become God’s people (Ex 6:7). Chapters 25–40: presence — “that I may dwell in their midst” (Ex 25:8); at the end the glory fills the tabernacle. Ex 19:4: God “brought you to myself”. The main character is the Lord.

6. Think

Retell (books closed): What are the three parts of Exodus? What happens in each, in one sentence? Why does God do all this? Use at least: rescue, covenant, presence, “brought you to myself”.

Suggested: 1–18 rescue — God brings his people out of slavery; 19–24 covenant — at Sinai they become God’s people; 25–40 presence — God commands the tabernacle and his glory fills it. God says he rescued them to bring them to himself.

Discern: A classmate says, “Moses saved Israel, so Moses is Christ.” Is he right?

Suggested: No. Moses is a man God used; his leading the people out is a shadow of Christ’s greater rescue. A shadow resembles the real thing but is not it. Moses himself needed God’s rescue (Ex 4:24–26) and did not enter Canaan.
