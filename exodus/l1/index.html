想一想：第 1 张书签（拯救）的钥匙经文，为什么不在 1–18 章里，而在 19 章？它说的“带来归我”，和后面两段有什么关系？Think: why is the key verse for “Rescue” in chapter 19, not in chapters 1–18? How does “brought you to myself” connect with the other two parts?
参考：Suggested answer: 19:4 是神站在西乃山上，回头总结前面 18 章做的事：他把百姓从埃及救出来，目的不是让他们自由地各走各路，而是“带来归我”。接下来的立约（归他作百姓）和会幕（他住在他们中间），正是“归我”的具体样子。所以这一节像一个合页，把三段连在一起。In 19:4 God, at Mount Sinai, looks back on everything in chapters 1–18. He rescued the people not so that each could go his own way, but to bring them to himself. The covenant (becoming his people) and the tabernacle (God dwelling among them) are what “brought to myself” looks like. So this verse is a hinge joining all three parts.

这三张书签留在圣经里，后面 21 课都会用到。Leave the bookmarks in your Bible. You will use them in all 21 lessons that follow.

想一想 · 复述Think · Retell

约 5 分钟about 5 min
一、复述1. Retell 课内 · 不看书in class · books closed
讲给一个没上过这课的人听：出埃及记分哪三段？每段一句话讲了什么？神做这一切是为了什么？Explain to someone who missed this lesson: what are the three parts of Exodus? What happens in each, in one sentence? Why does God do all this?
至少用上：拯救、立约、同在、“带来归我”。Use at least: rescue, covenant, presence, “brought you to myself”.
参考要点：Key points: 1–18 章是拯救：神藉着摩西把以色列人从埃及为奴之家救出来，过红海，在旷野供养他们。19–24 章是立约：在西乃山，神和他们立约，颁布十诫和约书，他们成为神的百姓。25–40 章是同在：神吩咐造会幕，虽有金牛犊的事，最后耶和华的荣光充满帐幕，神住在他们中间。神说他救他们，是要“带来归我”。Chapters 1–18 are rescue: through Moses, God brings Israel out of the house of slavery, through the sea, and feeds them in the wilderness. Chapters 19–24 are covenant: at Sinai God makes a covenant with them and gives the Ten Commandments and the Book of the Covenant; they become his people. Chapters 25–40 are presence: God commands the tabernacle; despite the golden calf, in the end the glory of the Lord fills the tabernacle and God dwells among them. God says he rescued them to bring them to himself.

想一想 · 辨析Think · Discern

课内或课后in class or after
二、辨析2. Discern
有同学说：“摩西救了以色列人，所以摩西就是基督。”他说得对吗？A classmate says, “Moses saved Israel, so Moses is Christ.” Is he right?
想一想：摩西和基督哪里相像？哪里不同？“影儿”是什么意思？Think: how are Moses and Christ alike? How are they different? What does “shadow” mean?
参考要点：Key points: 不对。摩西是神使用的人，神藉着他领百姓出埃及，这件事是基督更大拯救的影儿；影儿像那真实的东西，却不是它本身。摩西自己也需要神的拯救（第 5 课会读到出 4:24–26），后来他自己也没有进迦南。真正救人脱离黑暗权势的，是基督。No. Moses is a man God used; God led the people out of Egypt through him, and that event is a shadow of Christ's greater rescue. A shadow looks like the real thing but is not the thing itself. Moses himself needed God's rescue (Lesson 5 will read Ex 4:24–26), and he did not enter Canaan. The one who truly delivers us from the domain of darkness is Christ.
