求你显出你的荣耀Show Me Your Glory

神既然“有怜悯有恩典”，为什么又说“万不以有罪的为无罪”？If God is “merciful and gracious,” why does he also say he will “by no means clear the guilty”?

三段讲解，每段之前先在纸本圣经里查经、找答案，看完再对照；“在磐石穴中”和“山下迎接摩西”两个第一视角活动，“拼出这个名”背诵游戏；最后有 20 分钟的综合练习。可以分两次上：第一次做到“课本 · 读完第二节”，第二次从查经三开始。整节课请用纸本圣经，准备好它和一支笔。Three short talks — before each one you search your printed Bible, afterwards you compare — two first-person activities, “In the cleft of the rock” and “Meeting Moses at the camp,” a memory game, “Put the name together,” and a 20-minute review at the end. You can split it into two sittings: the first up to the textbook page after section 2, the second from Search 3. Use a printed Bible all through the lesson; have it and a pen ready.

想一想 · 复述Think · Retell

约 2 分钟about 2 min
一、复述1. Retell 课内 · 不看书in class · books closed
合上书，按“求看荣耀 → 宣告名 → 重新立约 → 脸发光”说出 33:18–34:35 的经过，每段至少说一件事。Books closed: retell 33:18–34:35 in four steps — Moses asks to see God’s glory → God proclaims his name → the covenant is renewed → Moses’ face shines — at least one event each.
参考要点：Key points: 求看荣耀——摩西求“显出你的荣耀给我看”；神说要显他一切的恩慈、宣告他的名；人见他的面不能存活，就把摩西放在磐石穴中，只让他看见背；吩咐他凿出两块石版。宣告名——耶和华在云中降临，宣告：有怜悯有恩典、不轻易发怒、有丰盛的慈爱和诚实、为千万人存留慈爱、赦免罪孽，万不以有罪的为无罪；摩西下拜，求神同行、赦免。重新立约——神说“我要立约”，不可与那地的居民立约，耶和华名为忌邪者；四十昼夜，十条诫写在版上。脸发光——摩西不知道脸发光，百姓怕挨近；他传完话就蒙上帕子，进到耶和华面前就揭去。Asking to see glory — Moses asked, “Please show me your glory”; God said he would make his goodness pass by and proclaim his name; no one can see his face and live, so he put Moses in the cleft of the rock and let him see his back; he told him to cut two tablets. The name — the LORD came down in the cloud and proclaimed: merciful and gracious, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness, keeping love for thousands, forgiving iniquity — yet by no means clearing the guilty; Moses worshiped and asked God to go with them and pardon them. The covenant renewed — “I am making a covenant”; no covenant with the people of the land; the LORD whose name is Jealous; forty days and nights; the Ten Commandments written on the tablets. The shining face — Moses did not know his face shone; the people were afraid; he spoke to them, then veiled his face, and removed the veil before the LORD.

想一想 · 辨析Think · Discern

约 3 分钟about 3 min
二、辨析2. Discern
有同学说：“神是爱，所以他不会追讨罪。”从 34:6–7 看，他说得对吗？至少引一处经文。A classmate says, “God is love, so he will not deal with sin.” From 34:6–7, is he right? Cite at least one passage.
想一想：34:6、34:7、20:5–6、罗 3:26。Think: 34:6, 34:7, 20:5–6, Rom 3:26.
参考要点：Key points: 只对了一半。神确实“有怜悯有恩典……为千万人存留慈爱，赦免罪孽”（34:6–7）；可是同一个名里也说“万不以有罪的为无罪”（34:7）。神的爱不是不管罪；新约说，神设立耶稣作挽回祭，“使人知道他自己为义，也称信耶稣的人为义”（罗 3:26）。Only half right. God is indeed “merciful and gracious… keeping steadfast love for thousands, forgiving iniquity” (34:6–7); but the same name says he “will by no means clear the guilty” (34:7). God’s love does not ignore sin; the New Testament says God put Jesus forward as a propitiation, “so that he might be just and the justifier of the one who has faith in Jesus” (Rom 3:26).
