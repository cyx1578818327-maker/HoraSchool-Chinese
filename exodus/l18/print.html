By what
Verse: the curtain is…

By the blood of Jesus; the curtain is his flesh (Heb 10:20).

Key points John 1:14: “The Word became flesh and dwelt among us” — literally pitched his tent. Jesus spoke of his body as the temple (John 2:19–21). When he died, the curtain was torn from top to bottom (Matt 27:51); by his blood we enter the holy places, through the curtain, that is, his flesh (Heb 10:19–20). The tabernacle was a copy and shadow (Heb 8:5); Christ is the reality.

6. Think

Retell (books closed): walking in from the east gate, name the six furnishings in order and where each stands.

Courtyard: bronze altar (before the door), basin (between tent and altar); Holy Place: table north, lampstand south, incense altar before the veil; Most Holy: the ark (mercy seat, two cherubim).

Discern: a classmate says, “In worship, as long as you are sincere, any way is fine.” From this lesson’s passages, is he right?

Only half right. God received only willing gifts (25:2), so sincerity matters; but he kept commanding “after the pattern shown you on the mountain” (25:9, 40; 26:30). How we come near God is for God to decide.
