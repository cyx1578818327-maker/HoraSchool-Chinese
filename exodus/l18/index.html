参考要点：Key points: 院子里：帐幕门前的燔祭坛（铜）；会幕和坛中间的洗濯盆（铜）。圣所里：北面的桌子（陈设饼）、南面的灯台、幔子外的香坛。至圣所里：约柜，柜上是施恩座和两个基路伯。In the courtyard: the bronze altar before the door; the bronze basin between the tent and the altar. In the Holy Place: the table (bread of the Presence) on the north, the lampstand on the south, the incense altar before the veil. In the Most Holy Place: the ark, with the mercy seat and two cherubim.

想一想 · 辨析Think · Discern

课内或课后in class or after
二、辨析2. Discern
有同学说：“敬拜神，只要心里真诚就好，用什么方式都可以。”从这一课的经文看，他说得对吗？A classmate says, “In worship, as long as you are sincere, any way is fine.” From this lesson’s passages, is he right?
想一想：出 25:9、40；26:30。下一课（32 章）会看到百姓“照自己的意思”造了什么。Think: Ex 25:9, 40; 26:30. Next lesson (ch. 32) shows what the people made “their own way”.
参考要点：Key points: 只说对了一半。真诚很重要，神也只收“甘心乐意”的（25:2）；但神一再吩咐“要照着在山上指示你的样式”（25:9、40；26:30）。怎样亲近神，是神来定，不是人来定。Only half right. Sincerity matters, and God received only willing gifts (25:2); but God repeatedly commanded, “after the pattern shown you on the mountain” (25:9, 40; 26:30). How we come near God is for God to decide, not us.
