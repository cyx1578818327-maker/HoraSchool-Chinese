金牛犊The Golden Calf

刚说“都必遵行”，怎么就造了金牛犊？They had just said “we will do.” How did it come to a golden calf?

三段讲解，每段之前先在纸本圣经里查经、找答案，看完再对照；“你是摩西”“跟摩西下山”两个第一视角活动；最后有 20 分钟的综合练习。可以分两次上：第一次做到“课本 · 读完第一节”，第二次从“你是摩西”开始。整节课请用纸本圣经，准备好它和一支笔。Three short talks — before each one you search your printed Bible, afterwards you compare — two first-person activities, “You are Moses” and “Down the mountain with Moses,” and a 20-minute review at the end. You can split it into two sittings: the first up to the textbook page after Talk 1, the second from “You are Moses.” Use a printed Bible all through the lesson; have it and a pen ready.

想一想 · 复述Think · Retell

约 2 分钟about 2 min
一、复述1. Retell 课内 · 不看书in class · books closed
合上书，按“山下 → 山上 → 下山”说出 32:1–30 的经过，每段至少说一件事。Books closed: retell 32:1–30 in three parts — down below → on the mountain → down the mountain — at least one event each.
参考要点：Key points: 山下——摩西迟延不下山，百姓要神像，亚伦用金环铸牛犊，“明日要向耶和华守节”。山上——神要灭绝他们，摩西用三个理由代求，耶和华不降那祸。下山——摩西摔碎法版、焚烧牛犊；亚伦推诿；利未人到摩西那里；第二天摩西说“或者可以为你们赎罪”。Down below — Moses delayed, the people asked for gods, Aaron cast a calf from gold rings, “a feast to the LORD.” On the mountain — God would consume them; Moses pleaded with three reasons; the LORD relented. Coming down — Moses broke the tablets and burned the calf; Aaron made excuses; the Levites came to Moses; next day Moses said, “perhaps I can make atonement.”
06

想一想 · 辨析Think · Discern

约 3 分钟about 3 min
二、辨析2. Discern
有同学说：“以色列人并没有拜别的神，他们说的是‘向耶和华守节’，所以问题不大。”从经文看，他说得对吗？A classmate says, “Israel didn’t worship another god — they said ‘a feast to the LORD,’ so it wasn’t a big problem.” From the text, is he right?
想一想：32:5、32:8、20:23、24:7。Think: 32:5, 32:8, 20:23, 24:7.
参考要点：Key points: 只看见了一半。他们确实用了耶和华的名（32:5），可是神说他们“快快偏离了我所吩咐的道”（32:8）。神已经说过“不可为自己作金银的神像”（20:23），百姓也答应过“都必遵行”（24:7）。照自己的意思敬拜耶和华，就是没有照神的约——和第 18 课“照着山上指示的样式”正好相反。He sees only half. They did use the LORD’s name (32:5), but God said they had “turned aside quickly out of the way that I commanded them” (32:8). God had said, “nor shall you make for yourselves gods of gold” (20:23), and the people had promised to obey (24:7). Worshiping the LORD their own way was not keeping God’s covenant — the opposite of Lesson 18’s “after the pattern shown on the mountain.”
