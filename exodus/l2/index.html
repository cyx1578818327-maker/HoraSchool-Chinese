想一想：作者为什么一遍遍重复“多起来”？Think: why does the writer keep repeating that they multiplied?
参考：Suggested answer: 重复是作者在强调。法老每想一次办法，经文就再说一次“多起来”，让读者看见：结局不在法老手里，而在那位应许亚伯拉罕的神手里。Repetition is the writer’s way of stressing something. Every time Pharaoh makes a plan, the text says again that they multiplied, so the reader can see who is really in control of the ending: not Pharaoh, but the God who made the promise to Abraham.

约 3 分钟about 3 min
一、复述1. Retell 课内 · 不看书in class · books closed
讲给一个没上过这课的人听：法老想了哪三条计？每一条的结果是什么？Explain to someone who missed this lesson: what were Pharaoh’s three plans? What was the result of each?
至少用上：苦工、收生婆、河、越发多起来。Use at least: hard labor, midwives, the Nile, “the more they multiplied”.
参考要点：Key points: ① 做苦工，建造比东和兰塞两座积货城 → 越发苦害他们，他们越发多起来（1:11–12）；② 吩咐希伯来收生婆杀男婴 → 收生婆敬畏神，存留男孩，百姓又多起来（1:15–20）；③ 下令把男孩丢在河里（1:22）→ 第 2 章神就在河里预备了摩西。每一次结果都和法老想的相反。① Hard labor building the store cities Pithom and Raamses → the more they were oppressed, the more they multiplied (1:11–12). ② The Hebrew midwives told to kill baby boys → the midwives feared God and let them live; the people multiplied again (1:15–20). ③ The order to throw the boys into the Nile (1:22) → in chapter 2 God prepares Moses in that very river. Every time, the result was the opposite of what Pharaoh wanted.

想一想 · 辨析Think · Discern

课内或课后in class or after
二、辨析2. Discern
有同学说：“第 1 章里神没有说话，也没有出场，所以这一章讲的只是以色列人自己的历史。”他说得对吗？A classmate says, “In chapter 1 God does not speak or appear, so this chapter is only Israel’s own history.” Is he right?
想一想：出 1:7 和创 15 的关系；出 1:17、20、21。Think: how Ex 1:7 relates to Gen 15; Ex 1:17, 20, 21.
参考要点：Key points: 不对。“多起来”正是神对亚伯拉罕的应许在成就（创 15:5、13–14；出 1:7）；收生婆因为敬畏神才存留男孩，神也厚待她们（出 1:17、20–21）。神没有说话，却一直在做工。No. Israel’s growth is God’s promise to Abraham coming true (Gen 15:5, 13–14; Ex 1:7). The midwives let the boys live because they feared God, and God dealt well with them (Ex 1:17, 20–21). God did not speak, but he was at work all along.
延伸讨论（给想多想的同学）To think further (optional)收生婆对法老说“希伯来妇人……收生婆还没有到，她们已经生产了”（1:19），这话不全是实话。神为什么还厚待她们？请仔细看经文说神厚待她们的理由是什么（1:20–21），经文有没有说神称赞她们说的话？和同学或老师讨论。The midwives told Pharaoh, “The Hebrew women… give birth before the midwife comes to them” (1:19), which was not the whole truth. Why did God still deal well with them? Look carefully at the reason the text gives (1:20–21). Does the text say God approved of what they said? Talk it over with a classmate or teacher.
