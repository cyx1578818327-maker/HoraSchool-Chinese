三、讲解一 · 七十个人，满了那地

要点 雅各全家下埃及时共 70 人（出 1:5）；到 1:7，他们“生养众多，并且繁茂，极其强盛，满了那地”。出埃及时步行的男人约 60 万（出 12:37）。这正应验神对亚伯拉罕的应许：后裔像天上的星（创 15:5），要寄居别人的地、受苦，后来带着许多财物出来（创 15:13–14）；神也对雅各说要使他在埃及成为大族（创 46:3）。神守约。

四、讲解二 · 法老的三条巧计

要点 不认识约瑟的新王怕以色列人“多”和“走”（1:9–10）。计一：做苦工，建比东和兰塞 → 越发苦害，越发多起来（1:11–12）。计二：吩咐收生婆施弗拉、普阿杀男婴 → 收生婆敬畏神，百姓又多起来（1:15–20）。计三：命众民把男孩丢在河里（1:22）→ 第 2 章揭晓。每次结果都与法老想的相反。

五、讲解三 · 敬畏神的人，和又一个王

要点 第 1 章只有两处提到神，都和收生婆有关：她们敬畏神，不照王的吩咐行（1:17）；神厚待她们，叫她们成立家室（1:20–21）。一千多年后，希律杀伯利恒两岁以里的男孩（太 2:16）；耶稣被带到埃及，应验“我从埃及召出我的儿子来”（太 2:15）。法老挡不住神的应许，希律挡不住神的儿子。

六、想一想

复述（不看书）：法老想了哪三条计？每一条的结果是什么？至少用上：苦工、收生婆、河、越发多起来。

参考：① 做苦工、建比东和兰塞 → 越发多起来（1:11–12）；② 吩咐收生婆杀男婴 → 收生婆敬畏神，存留男孩，百姓又多起来（1:15–20）；③ 下令把男孩丢在河里（1:22）→ 第 2 章神在河里预备了摩西。每次都与法老想的相反。

辨析：有同学说：“第 1 章里神没有说话，也没有出场，所以这一章讲的只是以色列人自己的历史。”他说得对吗？

参考：不对。“多起来”正是神对亚伯拉罕的应许在成就（创 15:5、13–14；出 1:7）；收生婆因敬畏神存留男孩，神也厚待她们（出 1:17、20–21）。神没有说话，却一直在做工。

延伸（选做）：收生婆对法老说的话（1:19）不全是实话。神为什么还厚待她们？经文给的理由是什么（1:20–21）？

3. Talk 1 · Seventy people, and a land filled

Key points Jacob’s family numbered 70 when they went down to Egypt (Ex 1:5); by 1:7 they “were fruitful and increased greatly… so that the land was filled with them.” About 600,000 men on foot left Egypt (Ex 12:37). This fulfils God’s promise to Abraham: offspring like the stars (Gen 15:5), strangers in another land, afflicted, then coming out with great possessions (Gen 15:13–14); and God told Jacob he would make him a great nation in Egypt (Gen 46:3). God keeps his covenant.

4. Talk 2 · Pharaoh’s three plans

Key points The new king, who did not know Joseph, feared that Israel would grow and go (1:9–10). Plan 1: hard labor building Pithom and Raamses → the more they were oppressed, the more they multiplied (1:11–12). Plan 2: the midwives Shiphrah and Puah told to kill baby boys → they feared God; the people multiplied (1:15–20). Plan 3: all the people told to throw the boys into the Nile (1:22) → see chapter 2. Each time, the opposite of what Pharaoh wanted.

5. Talk 3 · Those who feared God, and another king

Key points Only two places in chapter 1 mention God, both about the midwives: they feared God and did not obey the king (1:17); God dealt well with them and gave them families (1:20–21). More than a thousand years later Herod killed the boys of Bethlehem (Matt 2:16); Jesus was taken to Egypt, fulfilling “Out of Egypt I called my son” (Matt 2:15). Pharaoh could not stop God’s promise; Herod could not stop God’s Son.

6. Think

Retell (books closed): what were Pharaoh’s three plans, and the result of each? Use at least: hard labor, midwives, the Nile, “the more they multiplied”.

Suggested: ① hard labor building Pithom and Raamses → the more they multiplied (1:11–12); ② midwives told to kill baby boys → they feared God and let them live; the people multiplied (1:15–20); ③ boys to be thrown into the Nile (1:22) → in chapter 2 God prepares Moses in that river. Each time, the opposite of what Pharaoh wanted.

Discern: a classmate says, “In chapter 1 God does not speak or appear, so this chapter is only Israel’s own history.” Is he right?

Suggested: no. Israel’s growth is God’s promise to Abraham coming true (Gen 15:5, 13–14; Ex 1:7); the midwives let the boys live because they feared God, and God dealt well with them (Ex 1:17, 20–21). God did not speak, but he was at work.

Optional: what the midwives told Pharaoh (1:19) was not the whole truth. Why did God still deal well with them? What reason does the text give (1:20–21)?
