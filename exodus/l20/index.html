你若不亲自和我同去If Your Presence Will Not Go

地照样给，使者照样保护，只是神不同去——摩西为什么不接受？The land still given, the angel still protecting — only God will not go. Why would Moses not accept that?

三段讲解，每段之前先在纸本圣经里查经、找答案，看完再对照；“站在帐棚门口”第一视角活动和“如果你是摩西”预测；最后有 20 分钟的综合练习。可以分两次上：第一次做到“课本 · 读完第二节”，第二次从“如果你是摩西”开始。整节课请用纸本圣经，准备好它和一支笔。Three short talks — before each one you search your printed Bible, afterwards you compare — a first-person activity, “At your tent door,” an “If you were Moses” prediction, and a 20-minute review at the end. You can split it into two sittings: the first up to the textbook page after section 2, the second from “If you were Moses.” Use a printed Bible all through the lesson; have it and a pen ready.

想一想 · 复述Think · Retell

约 2 分钟about 2 min
一、复述1. Retell 课内 · 不看书in class · books closed
合上书，按“山上代求 → 神不同去 → 营外会幕 → 摩西再求”说出 32:30–33:17 的经过，每段至少说一件事。Books closed: retell 32:30–33:17 in four steps — Moses pleads → God will not go → the tent outside the camp → Moses asks again — at least one event each.
参考要点：Key points: 山上代求——摩西承认百姓犯了大罪，求神赦免，不然涂抹他的名；神说谁得罪我就涂抹谁，使者必在前面。神不同去——地、使者、流奶与蜜都给，只是“我自己不同你们上去”；百姓称为凶信，摘下妆饰。营外会幕——会幕在营外，离营却远；云柱降下，百姓在帐棚门口下拜；耶和华与摩西面对面说话。摩西再求——“你若不亲自和我同去，就不要把我们从这里领上去”；神说“我必亲自和你同去”。Moses pleads — he confesses the great sin, asks God to forgive or blot him out; God says whoever sins is blotted out, and his angel will go ahead. God will not go — land, angel, milk and honey given, only “I will not go up among you”; the people call it disastrous and remove their ornaments. The tent outside — far off from the camp; the cloud comes down; the people worship at their tent doors; the LORD speaks with Moses face to face. Moses asks again — “If your presence will not go with me, do not bring us up from here”; God says, “My presence will go with you.”
06

想一想 · 辨析Think · Discern

约 3 分钟about 3 min
二、辨析2. Discern
有同学说：“只要能进迦南地，又有使者保护，神去不去没那么要紧。”从经文看，他说得对吗？至少引一处经文。A classmate says, “As long as they get into Canaan and have an angel to protect them, whether God goes or not doesn’t matter much.” From the text, is he right? Cite at least one passage.
想一想：33:4、33:15、33:16、25:8。Think: 33:4, 33:15, 33:16, 25:8.
参考要点：Key points: 不对。百姓听见神不同去，把它叫作“凶信”（33:4）；摩西宁可不走：“你若不亲自和我同去，就不要把我们从这里领上去”（33:15）；以色列与万民的分别，就在于神与他们同去（33:16）。神本来就是要“住在他们中间”（25:8）。He is wrong. When the people heard God would not go, they called it a “disastrous word” (33:4); Moses would rather not go: “If your presence will not go with me, do not bring us up from here” (33:15); what made Israel distinct from every people was God going with them (33:16). God’s purpose all along was to “dwell in their midst” (25:8).
